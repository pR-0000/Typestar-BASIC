# Welcome! Printy is here to help

![Printy, our keyboard companion, welcomes you.](../images/printy-salut-v2.png)

An old typewriter, a few lines of BASIC, and here is a little computer ready to follow your ideas! **Printy** will be your companion in this manual. Together, you will learn to calculate, ask a question, draw on paper and build a game.

New to programming? This is a good time to try. **Start small, observe, then improve.** You will soon understand how several instructions can work together.

## Why learn BASIC today?

Programming teaches you to **organise a project from beginning to end**. You choose a result, divide the work into steps, name the information you need and check what happens. These habits also help when preparing a presentation, solving a problem or inventing the rules of a game.

BASIC exercises your **logic**. What should happen when the player's health reaches zero? How often should an action repeat? How should an unexpected answer be handled? You will learn to ask these questions and give them precise answers.

Artificial intelligence can suggest an approach or explain an error. **Understanding your program** lets you assess its suggestions, correct them and choose the result yourself. Checking that the program really carries out your idea is part of the pleasure of programming!

> **Printy's tip:** before trying a calculation, predict its result. Then compare it with the display. You already have a proper checking method.

## An idea, a plan, an experiment

![Printy plans a project: find an idea, organise the steps, check the result.](../images/printy-projet.png)

Imagine a guessing game. First display a question. Add the player's answer, then the rule that decides whether they have won. Scores, sound and printing can come later. **Get each small step working before adding the next one.**

An error is part of learning. A missing quotation mark or an unsuitable calculation becomes a chance to understand the machine a little better. Save a copy in Studio before a major change.

Your Typestar has one display line and a small memory. That is enough to discover how a computer reasons, and to complete a project you can explain from beginning to end. **Your turn: Printy is waiting for your instructions!**

To install BASIC, follow [the illustrated guide](02-installation-firmware.md#installation). If the machine is ready, go straight to [your first calculation](03-premiers-pas.md).
