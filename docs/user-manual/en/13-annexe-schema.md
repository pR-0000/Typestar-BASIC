# Appendix - CPU board schematic

This Canon drawing shows the Typestar 7 **CPU PCB 1/2, NH1-0159-04**. It connects microcontroller IC9, circuit IC5, the memories and connectors. AJD published the scan; the accompanying article preserves the document's context. [P1]

![Typestar 7 CPU board: overview of Canon's drawing.](../images/schema-cpu-restaure.png)

The crop removes unused margins. Grayscale and contrast processing reduces the paper tint, fold and coloured highlights, **preserving the scan's printed marks and legible inscriptions**.
